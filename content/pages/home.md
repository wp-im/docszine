# Docszine

## A magazine for the life of documents

Docszine is a small editorial system for people who read, make, and maintain documents. We publish essays, methods, field notes, and reviews about the structures that let knowledge remain usable.

The first issue is about the reading surface: the quiet layer between an idea and the person trying to understand it.

[docszine_note title="How to read this site"]Start with the current issue, move through an article's sections, then follow a topic when you want to cross the issue boundary.[/docszine_note]

## What we pay attention to

We look at the unglamorous decisions that shape a publication: the order of an archive, the width of a text column, the label on a link, the boundary between a theme and a plugin, and the moment a reader needs a way back.

Docszine is published with WordPress because a durable editorial system should remain legible to its editors. The site itself is part of the experiment.
