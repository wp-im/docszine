# Contact

Docszine welcomes corrections, source suggestions, and thoughtful disagreements.

For editorial notes, include the article title, the specific passage, and enough context for us to check the claim. For technical issues, include the page URL and the browser or device where the problem appeared.

Email: hello@wordpress.im

We do not promise that every message becomes a published response. We do promise to read carefully and to correct the public record when the evidence calls for it.
