The Classic Theme is sometimes described as an older way of working, as though age alone made it less capable. Its real distinction is architectural: templates, hooks, styles, menus, and content filters remain visible as separate pieces.

## Visible seams are useful

When a theme uses the standard template hierarchy, an editor or developer can locate the shape of a page. The seam between a single article and an Issue archive is understandable. The seam between a theme and a plugin is negotiable.

That clarity is not nostalgia. It is a practical advantage for a small publication that needs to change one part without rebuilding everything.

## The editor remains close to the document

Headings, paragraphs, images, captions, quotations, and tables are ordinary content. They can be edited, exported, and inspected without reverse-engineering a visual canvas.

## A living format

The Classic Theme is not a refusal of new tools. It is a decision to keep the document’s structure legible while the design evolves around it.
